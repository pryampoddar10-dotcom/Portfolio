Methodology How the model is built, in one page

Revenue is built bottom-up from an order-book waterfall (each year opens with the undelivered book, adds new intake and converts a share into recognised revenue) instead of a top-line growth-rate extrapolation. That revenue build feeds a full three-statement model (income statement, balance sheet, cash flow), which in turn feeds enterprise value under three methods (discounted cash flow, the venture-capital method and a forward EV/Revenue multiple), triangulated into a single weighted estimate. A driver sensitivity tornado shows how far each individual assumption alone can move enterprise value, bear to bull, rather than presenting a single point estimate as certain.
